# Privacy Policy for Echo NovaShift

**Effective Date:** November 15, 2026

Echo NovaShift ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application handles user information.

### 1. Overview & Offline Nature
Echo NovaShift is a standalone, single-player orbital reflex puzzle game designed to run entirely offline on your device. The game does not connect to any external servers, databases, or cloud storage to function.

### 2. Data Collection and Usage
* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, or share any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts).
* **No Tracking or Analytics:** The application does not utilize any third-party software development kits (SDKs), analytics tools, or advertising networks to track user behavior.
* **Local Storage:** Any game scores, streak records, and preference settings are stored strictly and securely on your local device storage. We have no access to this data.

### 3. Permissions
Echo NovaShift requires minimal to no special device permissions to operate. It does not require internet access, location access, camera, or microphone permissions.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Because we do not collect any data whatsoever, Echo NovaShift is completely safe for users of all ages, including children. 

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
